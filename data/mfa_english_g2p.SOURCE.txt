Packaged representation
-----------------------

The release archive's OpenFst vector graph was converted to compact,
little-endian records for a dependency-free Lua decoder. State arc counts are
derived from block bases plus 16-bit deltas; final states use a bitmap, rank
index, and dense weight array; and the high bits of destination states occupy
unused label bits.
Floating-point tropical weights were rounded to signed integers at 1/1024
precision. Graph topology, input/output labels, final states, weights, and
shortest-path semantics were retained. Pynini, OpenFst, Python, native code,
and speech-synthesis assets are not distributed or required on the KOReader
device.
