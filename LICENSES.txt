database metadata records the exact source revisions and input hash.


Montreal Forced Aligner English US ARPA G2P model (CC BY 4.0)
================================================================

The portable graph in data/mfa_english_g2p.bin is derived from the English US
ARPA G2P model published by Montreal Corpus Tools for Montreal Forced Aligner:

  Model catalog: https://mfa-models.readthedocs.io/en/latest/g2p/English/index.html
  Release: g2p-english_us_arpa-v2.0.0
  Archive: english_us_arpa.zip
  Archive SHA-256:
    f079ae88f792458fa7c123b256e5b86cc55c29ac2ffc457c673e6c60c36cd143
  Copyright/attribution: Montreal Corpus Tools and model contributors

Montreal Corpus Tools distributes the model under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International license:

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: the OpenFst vector graph was converted to a custom fixed-width,
little-endian format; floating-point tropical weights were rounded to signed
integers at 1/1024 precision; graph topology, labels, and shortest-path
semantics were retained. The plugin includes neither Pynini nor OpenFst code.
The dependency-free decoder and conversion tool are original plugin code under
the MIT license above. Complete dimensions, hashes, and build instructions are
in data/mfa_english_g2p.SOURCE.txt.
