CMU Flite US-English letter-to-sound model
==========================================

The portable decision-tree data in data/cmu_flite_lts.bin is derived from
CMU Flite, and the corresponding interpreter section in main.lua is an
adaptation of Flite's letter-to-sound runtime:

  Flite: https://github.com/festvox/flite
  Pinned revision: 6c9f20dc915b17f5619340069889db0aa007fcdc
  Upstream model:
    lang/cmulex/cmu_lts_model.c
    lang/cmulex/cmu_lts_model.h
    lang/cmulex/cmu_lts_rules.c
  Upstream interpreter:
    src/lexicon/cst_lts.c

Modifications are clearly marked in main.lua. The C decision trees and phone
table were re-encoded into a compact, architecture-independent binary format;
the relevant inference algorithm was adapted to Lua; speech synthesis, audio,
the compiled Flite runtime, and Flite's lexicon are not included. Exact input
hashes and the repeatable conversion command are in
data/cmu_flite_lts.SOURCE.txt.
