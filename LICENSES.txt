WikiPron-derived language-pack data (CC BY-SA 4.0)
===================================================

The bundled US and UK English broad-IPA records in
data/en/pronunciations.sqlite3 were extracted from the English Wiktionary by
WikiPron. Optional language packs built under data/<language-code>/ contain
corresponding Wiktionary records extracted by WikiPron:

  WikiPron: https://github.com/CUNY-CL/wikipron
  Bundled database release: v2.2.0
  Bundled database revision: d282e848a211ea31cfd730f0ced8bc8cdab9e83d
  English profiles: eng_latn_us_broad.tsv and eng_latn_uk_broad.tsv
  Wiktionary: https://en.wiktionary.org/
  Wiktionary attribution and license information:
  https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights

Those records and adaptations of them are available under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license:

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Attribution: Wiktionary contributors; extraction by the WikiPron project
(Jackson L. Lee, Lucas F.E. Ashby, M. Elizabeth Garza, Yeonju Lee-Sikka, Sean
Miller, Alan Wong, Arya D. McCarthy, and Kyle Gorman). No WikiPron program code
is bundled in this plugin.

The pronunciation database is a collection containing WikiPron/Wiktionary
records under CC BY-SA 4.0 and original curated records. Each record retains
its source label, and the
database metadata records the WikiPron release/tag, source revision, language,
profiles, record counts, and attribution. Individual WikiPron TSV files are
discovered from the selected stable release and are not SHA-256 pinned. The
final English SQLite artifact remains SHA-256 checked by the release tooling.


Montreal Forced Aligner English US ARPA G2P model (CC BY 4.0)
================================================================

The portable graph in data/en/g2p.bin is derived from the English US
ARPA G2P model published by Montreal Corpus Tools for Montreal Forced Aligner:

  Model catalog: https://mfa-models.readthedocs.io/en/latest/g2p/English/index.html
  Release: g2p-english_us_arpa-v2.0.0
  Archive: english_us_arpa.zip
  Archive SHA-256:
    f079ae88f792458fa7c123b256e5b86cc55c29ac2ffc457c673e6c60c36cd143
  Copyright/attribution: Montreal Corpus Tools and model contributors

Montreal Corpus Tools distributes the model under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International license:

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: the OpenFst vector graph was converted to a custom fixed-width,
little-endian format; floating-point tropical weights were rounded to signed
integers at 1/1024 precision; Unicode input labels and UTF-8 output phones were
retained along with graph topology and shortest-path semantics. The plugin
includes neither Pynini nor OpenFst code.
The dependency-free decoder and conversion tool are original plugin code under
the MIT license above. Complete dimensions, hashes, and build instructions are
in data/en/g2p.SOURCE.txt.

Optional G2P models are not part of the normal release. Anyone distributing an
additional `data/<language-code>/g2p.bin` must retain the license and
attribution of that model's source archive; the builder records its identity
and SHA-256 in the adjacent `g2p.SOURCE.txt`.
